Our story

Grown from a
flea-market table.

Kholo — meaning "grown" — started with one silkscreen table at a Braamfontein flea market. This is how it grew.

History

2022 → today
  • 2022

    A table at the flea market

    Two lifelong friends from Yeoville and Alexandra pool savings for a screen-printing kit and start selling hand-printed tees at Braamfontein weekend markets.

  • 2023

    First hoodie drop

    The Khulu Hoodie sells out in a weekend, and Kholo starts trading full-time through Instagram and recurring pop-ups.

  • 2025

    A studio of our own

    Kholo moves into a small Braamfontein studio, and starts working with its first outside stockists.

  • 2026

    Kholo goes online

    This website launches, giving Kholo a permanent home beyond social media and market stalls.

Mission
Make bold, locally made streetwear accessible to the people who grew us.
Vision

To become a South African streetwear label worn nationwide, without losing the Braamfontein market-table spirit it started with.

What we stand for

01

Locally made

Every piece is printed, cut or embroidered in Johannesburg, by people we know by name.

02

Priced to wear, not shelve

Streetwear should be worn every day, not saved for special occasions — so we keep pricing honest.

03

Built for our audience

We design with our own community of students and young creatives in mind, not a boardroom trend report.

Our community
Who we make this for

Young, local, and paying attention.

Kholo is built for young adults aged 16–30 — students and early-career creatives across Gauteng who follow local fashion and music culture, and want to wear something that was actually made where they live.
